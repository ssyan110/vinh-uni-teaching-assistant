说明：在这个部分，每题有一张图片，你会先听到一个问题，接着再听到(A)(B)(C)
三个选项，请根据图片提示从(A)(B)(C)中选出正确的答案。每题念两次，

Directions: In this test part, each test item comes with a picture. You will hear a question and three 
possible answers (A), (B), and (C) read aloud. Please use the picture to choose the most appropriate 
answer to each statement. Each test item will be read twice, and there will be a 5-second pause after the 
question is read for the first time. After the second reading, you will have five seconds to mark your 
answer before you hear the next test item. 
 
例题如下Example： 
 
你会看到一张图片You will see one picture： 
 
接着，你会听到一个问题和三个选项。 
Then, you will hear one question and three possible answers. 
 
第一题：眼镜在哪里？ 
(A) 在盒子里面。 
(B) 在盒子下面。 
(C) 在盒子上面。 
 
这一题的答案是(A)，请把答案涂在答案卡上。 
The correct answer to this test item is (A). Please fill in the corresponding circle on your answer sheet.

说明：在这个部分，每题有(A)(B)(C)三张图片，你会听到一段两个人的对话，一

dialogue between two speakers, with four sentences altogether. After each dialogue, someone will ask a 
question about the dialogue. Please choose the picture that best answers this question. Each dialogue will 
be read twice, with a 5-second pause in between each reading. After the second reading, you will have 
five seconds to mark your answer before a new test item begins.

第四部分 
Part Four 
(第46～50题) 
 
说明：在这个部分，你会听到两个人的对话，对话结束后有一个问题，每个
问题都有(A)(B)(C)(D)四个选项，这四个选项的内容也会出现在题本
上。请根据问题选出合适的答案。每题只念一遍，选项念完以后停五
秒，就继续下一题。 
Directions: In this part of the test , you will hear a dialogue between two speakers. After each  
dialogue, you will be asked a question. Each test item has four possible answers (A), (B), (C) 
and (D) listed on your answer sheet. For each question, please choose the best possible answer.  
Each test item will only be read once. You will have five seconds to mark your answer before a  
new test item begins. 
例题如下 Example： 
第四题 
    （男）：小美，妳的电话号码是几号？ 
    （女）：上次不是才给你吗？ 
    （男）：抱歉，我不小心把那张纸给弄丢了。 
    （女）：把你的手机拿出来吧！记在你的手机里，这样才不会再弄丢。 
    （男）：小美为什么要这位先生把手机拿出来？ 
(A) 她想知道对方有没有手机 
(B) 她想借对方的手机打电话 
(C) 她不相信对方把手机弄丢了 
(D) 她想把自己的电话号码给对方 
 
这一题的答案是(D)，请把答案涂在答案卡上。 
The correct answer to this test item is (D). Please fill in the corresponding circle on your  
answer sheet.
