# Teacher read-aloud and introduction activity ruling — 2026-11-04

## User decision

For future teacher-read-aloud listening activities, use a simple two-page sequence: the first page tells students to listen and gives a few answerable prompt questions; the second page asks them to introduce the information from their notes. Do not automatically add a listener check, challenge tier, listener follow-up question or presenter response. Add a question-and-response exchange only when the requested learning goal explicitly depends on information exchange, such as an interview or a recommendation task.
