## Final Direction

Use Lesson 01 as the visual source of truth: light classroom deck, soft teal/lavender accents, frosted menu bar, large Simplified Chinese, Vietnamese classroom instructions, pinyin support, and no PPTX workflow.

## Design Rules

- Use `slides/assets/slide-base.css` and `slide-base.js` copied from Lesson 01.
- Keep the top menu bar on teaching slides.
- Show printed textbook page only in `.page-indicator`, for example `Trang 76-77`.
- Vocabulary slides follow Lesson 01 `05-vocab-ni.html`: 16:9 image frame, pinyin, large Chinese, Vietnamese meaning, Hán Việt and word type.
- Whenever Chinese and pinyin appear together, align each pinyin chunk directly above the matching Chinese character or word group. Do not use one separate centered pinyin sentence for Chinese sentences with punctuation.
- Dialogue slides follow Lesson 01: avatar on the left, speech card on the right, only two dialogue lines per slide.
- Regular lessons do not include pinyin teaching modules.
- For non-template concept slides, center the main content vertically and horizontally when possible.
- Student-facing slides must not include teacher-only notes, internal labels, or source/tool explanations. Visible instructions, activity names, section titles, homework assignments, labels, and notes must be Vietnamese; Chinese is allowed only as target learning content. Check every HTML slide for text overflow inside the slide, background image panels, cards, bubbles, and tiles.

## Content Requirements Included

- Warmup: `你在哪儿？` with 教室, 家, 公司, 公园.
- Vocabulary: all 20 lesson vocabulary records, including sub-entries 办公、电、话、手.
- Vocabulary practice: multiple 16:9 matching slides, about 4 words per slide, with three independently shuffled rows. Do not label the rows; leave the slide blank for presenter drawing.
- Grammar: visual Chinese word-order explanation with blocks and plus icons.
- Grammar practice: Vietnamese-labeled error correction.
- Dialogue: two lines per slide, avatar + dialogue card.
- Culture: `请问` Vietnamese comparison plus common number meanings `520`, `666`.
- Hanzi writing: 住、有、公、室、在、家、呢、知、道、电、话、号、手、机.
