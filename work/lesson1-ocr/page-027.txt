Tone-marks should be placed on the main vowels. When the vowel i carries a
tone-indicator, the dot on i is removed, e. g. ni, bi. If there are two or more than two
vowels in a syllable, the tone-mark is placed on the one which requires a bigger (or
the biggest) opening of the mouth, e.g. hdo, mèi, lóu.
